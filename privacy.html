OTA X Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Welcome to OTA X. We respect your privacy and want you to understand how information may be collected, used, and protected when you use OTA X.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use OTA X, we may collect information such as:

  • Username and profile information
  • Email address or other account information you provide
  • Password information, stored securely in hashed form
  • Photos, videos, posts, comments, messages, and other uploaded content
  • Information provided when contacting support or sending feedback
  • Basic technical information needed to operate, secure, and improve the platform

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your OTA X account and provide platform features
  • Allow you to create, upload, share, and interact with content
  • Communicate with you about your account or platform updates
  • Respond to support requests and feedback
  • Protect OTA X users and the platform from abuse, fraud, and security threats

3. User Content & Visibility

When you upload content to OTA X, it may be visible to other users depending on your visibility settings. You should avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly unless you are comfortable with others seeing it.

4. Data Retention & Security

We take reasonable measures to protect account information. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service and comply with applicable obligations.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or account-related requests, please contact OTA X through the official support or feedback channel.
